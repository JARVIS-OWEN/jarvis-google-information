JARVIS Gmail Test is a private, personally operated assistant connection. This information website does not offer public account registration, a subscription or access to the assistant.

Operator control

The operator chooses which accounts to connect and which actions to authorise. AI-generated summaries and proposed actions may contain errors and should be checked where appropriate. Saving a task does not by itself prove a reminder was scheduled or delivered.

Connection access

Google access remains subject to the permissions the operator grants. The connection can be revoked through Google Account connections. The privacy notice describes how information is used and retained.
